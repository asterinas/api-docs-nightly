fn append_uevent_vars( &self, dev: &BusDevice<Self>, vars: &mut UeventVars, ) -> Result<()>

Appends bus-specific entries to a device’s uevent environment.

Called for automatic and explicit events and for uevent reads. Do not add DRIVER; the model derives it.

Must not bind, unbind, or remove the same device, or read or write its driver-provided attributes, because automatic bind/unbind events hold the device’s binding lock.
