Device uevents.

A uevent notifies user space of a device change, carrying a UeventAction, sysfs path, subsystem, sequence number, and KEY=VALUE environment entries (UeventVars). Events are serialized as action@devpath\0 followed by NUL-separated KEY=VALUE entries and sent to user space through Netlink group 1.

For bus and class devices, registration, removal, and driver binding or unbinding automatically emit Add, Remove, Bind, and Unbind. The other actions, such as Change, are never emitted automatically. Call emit to send an event with any action.

The device model fills in ACTION, DEVPATH, SUBSYSTEM, and SEQNUM, as well as MAJOR, MINOR, DEVNAME, DEVMODE, DEVTYPE, and DRIVER from the device’s number, node, type, and bound driver. Implementations of the Bus and Class traits can add entries to the uevents of their devices by implementing append_uevent_vars.

Every registered bus or class device has a read-write uevent sysfs attribute. Reading it returns the device’s KEY=VALUE entries as newline-separated lines without ACTION, DEVPATH, SUBSYSTEM, or SEQNUM. Writing to a bus or class device’s uevent attribute requests a synthetic event. The request format is <action> [<UUID> [<KEY=VALUE> ...]], following Linux’s Documentation/ABI/testing/sysfs-uevent.
