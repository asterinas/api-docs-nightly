The create-object recipe family.

One dispatcher routes on the fresh layer lookup of the target name: an absent or opaque-hidden name creates directly in the upper parent, a whiteout-hidden name replaces the whiteout through a completely prepared private workdir temp, and a fresh positive target fails with ESTALE. All three VFS create-family entries flow through one CreateOp, and SymLink can only enter through the atomic create_symlink entry.
